WHO IS IN YOUR MEDIA

People

Faces are found and grouped on this Mac. Name people to search for them and to see them in your video ideas.

Faces that belong to no one yet: passers-by, and faces the grouping was unsure of. Select some and link them to a person.

The sorter merges people whose faces are clearly one person and links faces that clearly belong to someone; anything less certain waits here. Faces you confirmed are never touched, and every change can be undone.

Faces come from the stored previews (520 px); items analysed from their originals get full-resolution faces. Fingerprints: AuraFace-v1 (Apache-2.0); detection: YuNet (MIT). Face data stays in this catalogue.

        Google Photos

        Import selected media with Google's Picker, or read metadata sidecars from a Takeout archive for a broader inventory.

        Google no longer permits an app to silently read your entire existing library. Picker access is selection-based. Download links expire; stable IDs and filenames remain in this catalogue.
        Open Google's Picker ↗

        Select files, press Done in Google, then import here. Expired selections need choosing again.

        Selection batches

        Batches survive app restarts. A newer selection can refresh access for the same items. Keep batches open while they have pending jobs.

        Photos and videos inside the archive are never extracted. Takeout IDs may differ from Picker IDs; explicit linking avoids false merges.
